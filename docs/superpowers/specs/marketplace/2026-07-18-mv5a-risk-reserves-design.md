# Marketplace MV5a — Risk, Reserves & Negative Balances (design)

**Status:** held / uncommitted draft for user hardening.
**Slice of:** the marketplace overview (`2026-07-16-multi-vendor-overview-design.md`, §MV5 "marketplace hardening"). MV5a bundles the three mutually-dependent financial items — **rolling reserves**, **provider-reported chargebacks**, and **negative-balance / debt** — because a chargeback *creates* a negative balance, reserves are the *policy tool* that absorbs it, and negative-balance recovery is the *mechanism*. They share one ledger and the `reserved` seam MV4 carved out. The other MV5 items (seller suspension, seller API keys/webhooks, residual security/pgsql hardening) are separate later slices. Import tooling is out (→ `thallo/packages/thallo-importers`).

**Builds on:** MV3 settlement ledger (`commerce_marketplace_ledger`, append-only, 12-field idempotency verify expanded by this slice to 14 with `reserve_uuid`/`chargeback_uuid`, `LedgerAccountLock`, `LedgerRepository::balanceComponents`), MV4 payout saga (`reserve_hold`/`reserve_release` with `payout_uuid`; the `reserved` component is `−(Σ reserve_hold + Σ reserve_release WHERE payout_uuid IS NULL)`), and the immutable per-line seller snapshots + `RefundService`/`LedgerPostingService::postRefund` proportional-reversal machinery.

**Default-off:** everything here is inert unless marketplace mode is enabled AND a non-zero reserve policy is configured / a chargeback event is ingested. With no policy and no chargebacks, MV5a posts nothing and existing behavior is byte-identical.

**Release consolidation (decided).** MV5a ships **bundled with MV4** in one coordinated, still-unpublished release train — there is **no Commerce 1.3.0 boundary, no migration 017, no upgrade migration**. Because contracts 1.5.0, payvia 2.1.0, and commerce 1.2.0 are all currently unpublished (the MV4 release is a pending user step), MV5a's additions consolidate into those same version numbers: **extension-contracts 1.5.0** (payout port + `ProviderChargebackEvent`), **payvia 2.1.0** (transfers + dispute-webhook mapping), **commerce 1.2.0** (MV4 payouts + MV5a reserves/chargebacks). **Marketplace migrations `010`–`016` first publish together in Commerce 1.2.0**, so every schema change here is a fold into an unreleased create-migration, never an ALTER.

---

## 1. Goal

Give the marketplace the money-safety machinery to (a) hold a **rolling reserve** out of each seller's proceeds and auto-release it on schedule; (b) ingest **provider-reported chargebacks** as durable, idempotent events and reverse the seller's settlement through the ledger; and (c) let a seller balance go **legitimately negative (debt)** when a clawback exceeds their funds, recovering it from future sales and freezing payouts until cleared — all as append-only ledger truth, never mutation.

---

## 2. Design

### 2.1 Rolling reserve policy (workspace default + per-seller override, snapshotted)
- The policy is two integers: **`reserve_bps`** (percentage in integer basis points) and **`reserve_days`** (hold duration in integer days). **`0` bps OR `0` days ⇒ no rolling reserve** (the whole feature is inert for that seller).
- Resolution order: **per-seller override** (`commerce_sellers.reserve_bps`/`reserve_days`, nullable) falls back to the **workspace default** (`commerce_marketplace_settings.reserve_bps`/`reserve_days`). A null per-seller value inherits; a `0` per-seller value explicitly disables (does NOT inherit).
- **Policy changes affect future sales only.** The resolved `(reserve_bps, reserve_days)` is **snapshotted onto each reserve hold at creation** and never recomputed. Changing the policy never touches existing holds — no historical recalculation, ever.
- Setting the policy is a `commerce:write` operator action; per-seller override is operator-set. Both write an append-only `commerce_reserve_policy_events` audit row in the **same transaction** as the policy mutation (the MV3 commission-policy audit pattern, not the commission-specific table).

### 2.2 Reserve hold at settlement (base = seller proceeds after commission, ex tax & shipping)
- When a seller order settles (`OrderPaymentService::markPaid` → `LedgerPostingService::postSale`, alongside `sale_credit`/`commission_debit`), and the resolved policy is non-zero, post a **`reserve_hold = −reserve_amount`** carrying `payout_uuid = NULL` (the risk-reserve discriminator), plus a durable `commerce_seller_reserves` row.
- **Reserve base = the seller's net merchandise proceeds after commission, excluding tax and shipping.** As built, `attributed_total = subtotal − allocated_discount + allocated_shipping + allocated_tax`, so it is **not** the reserve basis. Derive `merchandise_after_discount = subtotal − allocated_discount` (guardrail: it must equal `attributed_total − allocated_shipping − allocated_tax`), then `reserve_base = max(0, merchandise_after_discount − commission_amount)`. `allocated_shipping_discount` is never subtracted, matching MV2's free-shipping invariant.
- `reserve_amount = floor(reserve_base × reserve_bps / 10000)`. Integer minor units throughout; a zero result posts nothing (no ledger noise).
- The hold is **immutable** and carries a **`release_at`** timestamp = the persisted seller-order **`confirmed_at`** + `reserve_days` (the settlement timestamp written immediately before `postSale()` in `markPaid()`), plus the snapshotted `reserve_bps`/`reserve_days`. The durable row is keyed to the exact `seller_order_uuid`; its ledger hold carries `reserve_uuid`. Idempotency key `{order_uuid}:{seller_uuid}:reserve_hold` (one hold per seller-order settlement). On replay, the existing reserve row is re-read and its seller/order/currency/original amount/policy snapshots/held-at/release-at are verified exactly before the ledger replay is accepted; current policy and current time are never recomputed into a second verdict.

### 2.3 Reserve release sweep (scheduled, idempotent, per-hold)
- A CLI sweep (`commerce:marketplace:reserves:release-sweep`, host-cron-invoked, no commerce scheduler — MV4 sweep pattern) selects `commerce_seller_reserves` rows that are `status=held AND release_at IS NOT NULL AND release_at ≤ now`. For each it claims the seller/currency account lock, re-reads the reserve, and derives `remaining = max(0, −Σ(reserve_hold + reserve_release))` by `reserve_uuid`. It posts **only the positive remaining amount** as `reserve_release` (`payout_uuid = NULL`, `reserve_uuid` set), then marks the row `status=released, closed_at=now`. Idempotency key `{reserve_uuid}:scheduled_release` — a replay verifies the existing entry, never double-releases. A reserve partially consumed by earlier liabilities therefore releases only its remainder; a compensating re-hold (§2.10) is naturally included in the same derived amount.
- Independent per row (one seller/currency failure never aborts the sweep). A released reserve returns to `available` and drops out of `reserved`.

### 2.4 Chargeback ingestion (provider-reported, durable-event-first, idempotent)
- A **neutral provider chargeback event** (§4) carries the resolved **tenant**, **provider**, **provider event ID**, Payvia's durable **payment reference**, the ownership-resolved **`PayableReference`** (type/id/amount/currency), chargeback **amount**, **currency**, **reason code**, and **occurred-at**. Commerce accepts only its supported order payable type and validates that payable id, tenant, amount/currency bounds, and provider/payment correlation are coherent. Marketplace posting is governed by the order's immutable `marketplace_partitioned` marker, never current workspace activation: a historical partitioned order still processes after deactivation, while a non-partitioned order is outside MV5a and produces no marketplace chargeback/ledger row. Commerce does NOT reproduce the provider's dispute system — no evidence, deadlines, or opened→won/lost workflow (all deferred).
- Ingestion order (load-bearing): insert the `commerce_chargebacks` row first inside the processing transaction; its unique `(tenant, provider, provider_event_id)` is the idempotency claim. On conflict, Commerce re-reads and verifies the immutable identity/payload fields exactly; a conflicting reuse is an integrity error, never a silent skip. A fully attributable event then writes its attribution rows, ledger postings, and `status=posted` in that **same transaction**, eliminating a committed partial-posting window. A partial event without line attribution commits as `awaiting_attribution`; an unresolvable/mismatched event commits as `integrity_hold`. A repair path handles explicit non-posted states, but never guesses attribution.
- Delivery boundary: Payvia persists and signature-verifies its provider webhook, uses a narrow trusted system-correlation query to resolve the provider transaction to exactly one persisted `payments` row, and constructs the event's tenant + `PayableReference` from that authoritative row (never from webhook metadata alone). Zero or multiple matches fail closed. It then dispatches the exact contracts event through Glueful's event system. Commerce registers a listener that invokes the same ingestion service used by the authenticated operator/system endpoint. Listener failure must leave Payvia's durable provider event redispatchable; acceptance tests pin this redelivery behavior. Commerce never imports a Payvia class.

### 2.5 Chargeback attribution & ledger reversal (proportional, reserve-first, commission reversal)
- Attribution is through the **immutable order-line seller snapshots** (never live seller data). Resolve the disputed charge to its order, then to the seller lines.
- **Partial chargebacks REQUIRE explicit persisted line attribution.** A provider event that lacks it remains `awaiting_attribution`; an operator supplies `(order_line_uuid, amount)` rows whose sum must equal the chargeback amount before posting. A **full chargeback** (equal to the original charge) MAY **auto-expand** and persists the generated attribution rows. Expansion is deterministic because shipping and shipping tax live at seller-order level: for each immutable seller order, distribute its `attributed_total` across its immutable lines with the shared `LargestRemainder` helper, weighted by `max(0, line_total − discount_amount + tax_amount)` and tie-broken by line UUID; if every weight is zero, use equal unit weights. The resulting line amounts sum exactly to each seller order's `attributed_total`, and all sellers sum exactly to the parent charge. No attribution may exceed the derived original line/seller amount remaining after earlier chargebacks/refunds.
- Per attributed seller, reverse settlement **proportionally**, mirroring `postRefund`:
  - **`chargeback_debit = −(seller-attributed chargeback amount)`** — this reverses the same attributed proceeds credited at sale, including attributable shipping/tax rather than silently making the marketplace fund them. Key `{chargeback_uuid}:{seller_uuid}:chargeback_debit`.
  - **`commission_reversal = +(seller commission share)`** — commission reversal uses the existing refund-style cumulative merchandise-basis cap, so tax-inclusive or oversized line attribution cannot over-reverse commission. Key `{chargeback_uuid}:{seller_uuid}:commission_reversal`. Net seller liability = attributed debit minus commission reversal.
- **Reserve-first consumption:** a shared `ReserveConsumptionService` is used by both chargebacks and refunds. Under the seller/currency lock, consume `min(net seller liability, current_reserved)` from held reserves **earliest-`release_at`-first (FIFO; indefinite manual holds sort last)**. Partial consumption is first-class: each affected reserve gets its own `reserve_release` ledger row carrying `reserve_uuid` plus `chargeback_uuid` or `refund_uuid`, and stays `held` while it has a positive remainder. A reserve becomes `consumed` only when liability releases exhaust it. Idempotency key `{liability_kind}:{liability_uuid}:{seller_uuid}:{reserve_uuid}:reserve_release`. The reserve can never be over-released (`reserved ≥ 0`; each release ≤ the locked, re-read reserve remainder).
- **Unattributable amount is marketplace-funded, never silently assigned** to a seller: any portion of the chargeback that resolves to no seller line (or exceeds total seller allocations) posts against the marketplace account, explicitly.
- **A later provider reversal / dispute win is a SEPARATE compensating event** (§2.10) keyed by its own provider event ID — never a mutation or deletion of the original chargeback rows.

### 2.6 Negative balance & debt (derived, in full, no separate balance)
- The ledger must record the **complete** financial event even when the seller cannot cover it — refusing or truncating a chargeback would make Commerce disagree with the provider (money-conservation).
- Sequence: **consume/release eligible reserves against the liability first** (§2.5), then **post the remaining debit in FULL, allowing `available` to go negative.**
- **There is no separate mutable "debt balance."** Debt is simply the negative derived ledger balance. **`debt = max(0, −available)`**, surfaced separately for seller/operator clarity (§2.9). Debt **clearance is derived** when `available` returns to `≥ 0` — no manual state transition, no flag.
- **Future `sale_credit`s automatically reduce a negative balance** through ordinary ledger arithmetic (a settling sale credits the seller, netting against the debt).
- **In-flight provider payouts are NOT implicitly cancelled** by a debt: an executing MV4 payout stays governed by its durable `reserve_hold`/provider state and its own saga; a chargeback arriving mid-payout interleaves under the shared account lock but never reaches in and cancels a committed payout attempt.

### 2.7 Payout eligibility & batch changes (available > 0, no outstanding debt)
- **Provider and manual payouts require `available > 0` and no outstanding debt** (`debt == 0`). This tightens MV4's `available ≥ amount` reserve check: a payout may not be created while the seller is in debt, and never draws `available` below zero.
- **Scheduled batches (`payouts:run-batch`) skip indebted sellers** (and any seller whose locked `available ≤ 0`), same independent-per-candidate discipline.
- `reserved` remains **separately visible** and is NOT payout-eligible — payouts continue to derive strictly from `available`.

### 2.8 Manual operator adjustments (audited; never mutation)
- **Emergency reserve hold/release** remains possible as an audited operator action, but never as an untracked raw ledger post. A manual hold requires a caller `Idempotency-Key`, non-empty actor and reason, creates a `commerce_seller_reserves` row with `source_kind=manual`, nullable `seller_order_uuid`, policy snapshots `0/0`, no automatic `release_at`, actor, reason, and the idempotency key; its ledger entry carries `reserve_uuid`. An exact replay returns the already-created hold, while a reused key with a different seller/currency/amount/reason is a conflict. Manual release names a reserve, requires/records the actor, and uses the same locked remaining-amount logic. This **does not replace** rolling policy; it layers on top.
- **Operator debt forgiveness** is an **explicit, audited positive `adjustment`** (credits the seller to lift them out of debt) and requires the existing `AdjustmentService` idempotency key — **never** deletion or mutation of chargeback/ledger entries. The chargeback rows are immutable history; forgiveness is a new compensating credit.

### 2.9 Balance components (add `debt`)
- `SellerBalanceService::balance()` / `LedgerRepository::balanceComponents()` gain **`debt = max(0, −available)`** alongside the existing `available`, `pending`, `reserved`, `paid_out`. `available`, `pending`, `paid_out` semantics are unchanged; `reserved` is unchanged (`−(Σ reserve_hold + Σ reserve_release WHERE payout_uuid IS NULL)`) but is now actually populated by risk holds. Derived in the same single grouped scan — no extra round-trip.

### 2.10 Compensating events (provider reversal / dispute win)
- A provider-reported **reversal of a chargeback** (the seller won, or the provider re-credited) arrives as its **own** provider event (its own event ID, referencing the original chargeback), and posts a dedicated **`chargeback_credit`** plus the matching **`commission_debit`** re-application. It **never mutates or deletes** the original chargeback rows. Over-credit / out-of-order / duplicate is guarded by the same event-ID idempotency + an integrity check (cumulative compensation may not exceed the original chargeback's postings — over-amount is an integrity finding, never a fabricated positive post), mirroring MV4's reversal-integrity discipline. If the original chargeback consumed a rolling reserve whose `release_at` is still in the future, the compensation also posts an idempotent `reserve_hold` against that same `reserve_uuid` for the amount previously consumed by this chargeback (capped so the reserve's derived remaining never exceeds its original amount) and reopens the row to `held`. If the window has elapsed, nothing is re-held.

### 2.11 Coupling & continuity
- **Contract-only coupling.** Commerce depends only on the neutral chargeback contract VO; it never names or reads payvia tables. No commerce→payvia composer dependency. If a future dispute-status query is added it is a soft-bound port (MV4 `container->has()` precedent).
- **Off-invariance (§7).** With marketplace off, or reserve policy `0`, or no chargeback ingested: no reserve holds, no chargeback rows, no new routes exposed, the manual/refund/payout paths byte-identical, and unrelated request paths run zero new queries. As in MV2, an already-partitioned historical order remains governed by its immutable marker even after current workspace deactivation; that maintenance obligation is not an ordinary off-mode path.

## 3. Schema — migrations `010`–`016` first publish together in **Commerce 1.2.0** (bundled with MV4), so every change is a fold into an unreleased create-migration; no ALTER/017/upgrade migration.

### 3.1 Reserve policy columns (folded into migration `010_CreateMarketplaceSellerTables`)
Both target tables are created in `010`, so both folds land there.
- `commerce_marketplace_settings`: add **`reserve_bps` INT NOT NULL DEFAULT 0**, **`reserve_days` INT NOT NULL DEFAULT 0** (workspace default; `0` = off).
- `commerce_sellers`: add **`reserve_bps` INT NULL**, **`reserve_days` INT NULL** (per-seller override; NULL = inherit workspace, `0` = explicitly off).

### 3.2 `commerce_seller_reserves` (new migration 015) — durable rolling-reserve holds
`id` bigint PK; `uuid` varchar(12); `tenant_uuid` varchar(12) default `''`; `seller_uuid` varchar(12); `currency` varchar(3); `source_kind` varchar(16) (`rolling | manual`); `seller_order_uuid` varchar(12) NULL (required for rolling, null for manual); `idempotency_key` varchar(128) NULL (required for manual, null for rolling); `amount` bigint (original held amount, > 0); `reserve_bps_snapshot` int; `reserve_days_snapshot` int; `status` varchar(16) default `held` (`held | released | consumed`); `held_at` timestamp; `release_at` timestamp NULL (required for rolling, null for indefinite manual); `closed_at` timestamp NULL; `created_by` varchar(12) NULL; `reason` varchar(255) NULL; `created_at`/`updated_at`. Remaining amount is derived as `max(0, −Σ(reserve_hold + reserve_release))` over ledger entries carrying this `reserve_uuid`; it is not a second mutable balance. The net may never exceed the row's original `amount`. Unique `(tenant_uuid, seller_order_uuid, seller_uuid)` (service-enforced rolling uniqueness with the DB unique as backstop where the nullable key participates), `(tenant_uuid, idempotency_key)` (manual-hold replay arbiter; nullable rolling rows remain exempt), `(tenant_uuid, uuid)`; index `(tenant_uuid, status, release_at)` (release sweep), `(tenant_uuid, seller_uuid, currency, status, release_at)` (FIFO consumption).

The same migration creates append-only **`commerce_reserve_policy_events`**: `id`, `uuid`, `tenant_uuid default ''`, `subject_kind` (`workspace|seller`), `subject_uuid`, `actor_uuid`, `before_policy` JSON, `after_policy` JSON, `created_at`; unique `(tenant_uuid, uuid)` and subject/time index. The policy write and audit insert share one transaction.

### 3.3 `commerce_chargebacks` (new migration 016) — durable provider chargeback events
`id` bigint PK; `uuid` varchar(12); `tenant_uuid` varchar(12) default `''`; `provider` varchar(32); `provider_event_id` varchar(191); `payment_reference` varchar(191) (the disputed charge/order); `order_uuid` varchar(12) NULL (resolved); `amount` bigint; `currency` varchar(3); `reason_code` varchar(64) NULL; `occurred_at` timestamp; `kind` varchar(16) default `chargeback` (`chargeback | reversal` — §2.10 compensating events share the table); `related_chargeback_uuid` varchar(12) NULL (a reversal points at its original); `status` varchar(24) default `received` (`received | awaiting_attribution | posted | integrity_hold`); `posted_at` timestamp NULL; `created_at`/`updated_at`. **Unique `(tenant_uuid, provider, provider_event_id)`** (idempotency authority); index `(tenant_uuid, order_uuid)`, `(tenant_uuid, status)` (explicit non-posted-state repair).

The same migration creates **`commerce_chargeback_lines`**: `id`, `uuid`, `tenant_uuid default ''`, `chargeback_uuid`, `order_line_uuid`, `seller_uuid`, `amount`, timestamps; unique `(tenant_uuid, chargeback_uuid, order_line_uuid)`, `(tenant_uuid, uuid)`; index `(tenant_uuid, seller_uuid)`. These rows are the durable attribution authority for partial events and the persisted expansion result for full events.

### 3.4 Ledger entry types
Fold nullable **`reserve_uuid`** and **`chargeback_uuid`** correlation columns + indexes into unreleased migration `012_CreateMarketplaceLedgerTables`; extend `LedgerRepository`'s insert/phpdoc and immutable replay verifier from 12 to **14 fields** accordingly. Reuses existing `reserve_hold`/`reserve_release` (`payout_uuid = NULL` for risk), `commission_reversal`, `commission_debit`, and `adjustment`. Adds closed entry types **`chargeback_debit`** and **`chargeback_credit`**. Every rolling/manual risk hold and release carries `reserve_uuid`; chargeback debit/credit and its commission entries carry `chargeback_uuid`. `payout_uuid` stays NULL on all risk-reserve entries so MV4's `reserved`/`pending` split holds.

## 4. Contract additions (extension-contracts **1.5.0**, consolidated with the MV4 payout port)
- New neutral inbound event **`ProviderChargebackEvent extends BaseEvent`** in `src/Payments/`, dispatchable through Glueful's event system: readonly `(string $tenantUuid, string $provider, string $providerEventId, string $paymentReference, PayableReference $payable, int $amount, string $currency, ?string $reasonCode, string $occurredAt, string $kind = 'chargeback', ?string $relatedEventId = null)`, with basic invariants (provider/event/reference/currency non-empty, amount > 0, event currency matches the payable currency, occurred-at parseable, kind ∈ {chargeback, reversal}, a `reversal` requires `relatedEventId`). `tenantUuid` may be the sentinel `''` in single-store Payvia mode; Commerce still validates ownership and amount bounds. This is an **inbound event**, not an outbound collector — Commerce receives it; it does not call the provider. A future dispute-status query port is deferred.
- Because contracts 1.5.0 is still unpublished, this event lands in the **same 1.5.0 release** as the MV4 payout port — not a separate 1.6.0.

## 5. Package / release order (framework seam + one bundled MV4 + MV5a train)
- **framework 1.71.0** (new `EventService::dispatchOrFail()` strict-dispatch seam) → **extension-contracts 1.5.0** (MV4 payout port + `ProviderChargebackEvent`) → **payvia 2.1.0** (MV4 transfers/payout + the dispute-webhook mapping) → **commerce 1.2.0** (MV4 payouts + MV5a reserves/chargebacks/accounting). Contracts/payvia/commerce are unpublished; framework 1.71.0 is a new framework release. The pin bumps + `extra.glueful.version` repair + Release commits + tags are the **USER's release step**.
- **Strict dispatch (framework 1.71.0):** the framework event bus fault-isolates listener exceptions (`EventDispatcher::dispatch` catch+log, never rethrow), so a failing Commerce chargeback listener would be swallowed and the durable payvia event marked dispatched — silently losing a real chargeback. Framework 1.71.0 adds `EventService::dispatchOrFail()` (log then RETHROW the original, STOP after the first failing listener; at-least-once → listeners MUST be idempotent). Payvia dispatches ONLY `ProviderChargebackEvent` via `dispatchOrFail` and marks the `provider_events` row dispatched only after it returns successfully, so a listener failure leaves the row retryable and `relayPending()` redelivers exactly once on success. Ordinary payvia events keep fault isolation. `dispatch()` is unchanged.
- **Payvia dispute-webhook mapping** (Stripe/Paystack dispute events → `ProviderChargebackEvent`) IS in scope, folded into the unpublished **payvia 2.1.0** (decided). Commerce's accounting stays independently testable against the normalized VO with a fake source, so the payvia mapping is a thin translator, never a commerce dependency.
- Payvia adds a narrow payment-owner correlation method alongside its existing subscription-only `ProviderCorrelationRepository`: locate exactly one `payments` row by trusted gateway transaction identity, then emit that row's persisted `tenant_uuid`, `reference`, and `PayableReference`. Missing, duplicate, or contradictory ownership fails closed and leaves the durable provider event retryable; Commerce independently revalidates tenant/payable/order correlation.
- Vendor-first dev: mirror the new contract VO into each repo's `vendor/glueful/extension-contracts/src/Payments/` locally (LOCAL COMPILE AID, **never staged**).

## 6. Surfaces
- **Operator (`commerce:write`, marketplace-enabled):** set workspace reserve policy; set per-seller reserve override; ingest a chargeback event (system/operator ingestion); emergency reserve hold/release; audited debt-forgiveness adjustment. Read: a seller's reserve holds + debt.
- **Seller (`commerce_seller:commerce.seller.reports.read`, own seller only):** read own `reserved` + upcoming reserve releases (amount + `release_at` only) and own `debt`. Sanitized — no provider event internals, no `payment_reference`/`provider_event_id` leaked (allow-list projection, MV4 leak-prevention discipline). No mutation. The existing `commerce.seller.payouts.read` remains specific to payout-account/payout-list surfaces.
- **No operator "reverse a chargeback" / clawback-initiation endpoint** — reversals are provider-reported only (§2.10).

## 7. Off-invariance (regression gates)
- Marketplace off / reserve policy `0` / no chargeback: zero reserve holds, zero chargeback rows, route manifest identical, manual+refund+payout JSON byte-identical, zero new queries on unrelated paths, folded defaults (`reserve_bps=0`, `reserve_days=0`) keep every existing seller reserve-free.

## 8. Testing (highlights)
- Rolling reserve: exact basis uses `subtotal − allocated_discount − commission_amount` and proves the equivalent `attributed_total − allocated_shipping − allocated_tax − commission_amount`; bps/days snapshot immutable across policy change; settlement replay verifies the persisted hold against `confirmed_at` without recalculating current policy/time; 0-bps/0-days inert; release sweep idempotent + FIFO; partial liability consumption leaves the correct remainder; `reserved` reflects unreleased holds only; manual indefinite holds never auto-release; caller-idempotent manual holds cannot double-reserve.
- Chargeback: tenant/provider/payment/`PayableReference` correlation; zero-or-multiple Payvia payment matches fail closed; Payvia durable-event redelivery when the Commerce listener fails; event-first idempotency claim with exact conflict verification; duplicate + out-of-order delivery = one posting set; partial without lines persists `awaiting_attribution`; attribution sum/caps enforced; full auto-expands seller totals to lines with stable largest-remainder allocation (including all-zero-weight fallback) and persists them; seller debit includes attributable shipping/tax while commission reversal remains merchandise-capped; unattributable → marketplace-funded; reserve-first consumption shared with refunds (FIFO, per-reserve correlation, `reserved ≥ 0`); a won dispute re-holds only the still-unexpired reserve amount consumed by that chargeback.
- Negative balance: reserve-first then full debit → `available` negative; `debt = max(0,−available)`; future `sale_credit` nets debt down; payout refused while debt/available≤0; batch skips indebted; in-flight payout not cancelled; compensating reversal is a separate credit, never mutation; over-amount reversal = integrity finding.
- **Live pgsql concurrency (MV4 fixture-child harness):** chargeback vs payout reservation under the shared seller/currency account lock (no double-spend, no over-release of reserve, debt/available consistent); concurrent reserve-release sweep vs chargeback consumption of the same hold (one wins, no double-release).

## 10. Resolved decisions
- **Fold-timing — RESOLVED:** MV5a ships **bundled with MV4 as Commerce 1.2.0**; migrations `010`–`016` first publish together in that release. Reserve-policy columns **fold into `010`**; `commerce_seller_reserves` = **015**, `commerce_chargebacks` = **016**. **No migration 017, no ALTER/upgrade migration, no Commerce 1.3.0 boundary.**
- **Payvia mapping — RESOLVED:** included in the unpublished **payvia 2.1.0** (thin dispute-webhook → `ProviderChargebackEvent` translator).
- **Contract release — RESOLVED:** `ProviderChargebackEvent` consolidates into the unpublished **extension-contracts 1.5.0** (not a new 1.6.0).

- **Reserve base — RESOLVED against source:** `attributed_total` includes allocated shipping and tax. - **Partial reserve consumption — RESOLVED:** ledger entries carry `reserve_uuid`; each liability releases individual reserves FIFO and may partially consume one. Remaining is derived, not a mutable balance; multiple liabilities are represented without a lossy scalar `consumed_by`.
- **Chargeback attribution — RESOLVED:** partial events without explicit lines persist as `awaiting_attribution`; full events auto-expand and persist generated lines. Seller debit reverses the attributed amount (including attributable shipping/tax), while commission reversal remains capped to merchandise basis.
